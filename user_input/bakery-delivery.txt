I run a small bakery in central Amsterdam and need to deliver fresh bread and pastries to my café customers every morning. I have a fleet of delivery vans — some are refrigerated and some are not. Certain products, like cream pastries and custard tarts, need to stay refrigerated during transport, so those deliveries must be assigned to a cooled van. Each van has a maximum weight it can carry. Some of my larger cafés also require a minimum drop-off quantity, so it's not worth sending a van there unless it's carrying at least that amount for them.
All deliveries must be completed between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM. Each café has its own time window when staff are available to receive a delivery — if we arrive before they open, the driver must wait, but we should try to avoid that since it wastes time. A few cafés have said they absolutely cannot receive deliveries after a certain time, so those windows are hard constraints.
Some roads in the city centre have weight restrictions in the morning, so the heavier vans cannot use certain routes. I also have two drivers who are only available from 7:00 AM, so the vans they drive cannot be dispatched before then.
I want to minimise total driving distance across all vans. Every café must be visited — no deliveries can be skipped. Each café is visited exactly once.
