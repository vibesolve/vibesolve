I own a chain of restaurants that need a food (ingredients) delivery schedule. I have two types of restaurants, lunch-based and dinner-based restaurants. All of them are scattered throughout the same city. The restaurants need 3 types of delivered goods: meat, vegetbles, and drinks. Every of these ingredients is delivered in separate vehicles, one vehicle per ingredient. The vehicles have different starting points. The catch is that vehicles have limited capacity, so they will sometimes need to return to their starting points to fetch more ingredients before continuing delivery. When delivering goods, these vehicles also need to pick up stuff (e.g. empty bottles) from these restaurants. This impacts the capacity as well. Lunch-based restaurants have priority, and need to get their goods by noon, while dinner-based restaurants can wait a bit longer. I need the optimal vehicle routes to deliver all goods to restaurants. Restaurants don't need all ingredients every day, so some restaurants might only need meat that day, or they could need all ingredients.
